Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Your privacy is fundamental to WebDrop Pro. This policy outlines how data is handled when you use our service. By using WebDrop Pro, you consent to the practices described below.

1. Direct Peer-to-Peer Transfers

WebDrop Pro is designed around privacy. File contents transferred through the platform travel directly between your device and the connected peer using end-to-end encrypted WebRTC streams. We do not upload, scan, or store your transferred files on any server. Files exist only on the sending device, the receiving device, and the encrypted link between them.

2. Signaling and Device IDs

To establish a connection between devices, temporary signaling data (such as randomized Device IDs and WebRTC connection metadata) is processed in real time to locate peers on the same local network or to pair devices via a paired ID. This metadata is transient and is discarded once connection signaling completes. No persistent user account or identity record is created.

3. QR Pairing Sessions

When you initiate a QR pairing, a short-lived session record is created on our backend containing only a randomly generated session identifier, a status flag, and a timestamp. This record exists solely to coordinate the pairing handshake and is treated as expired after 15 minutes. The record does not contain your name, email, file names, file contents, or any other personal data.

4. Local Storage and Cookies

We use lightweight local browser storage strictly for necessary site functions, such as remembering your user interface preferences (for example, theme choice, device name label, or cookie consent acceptance). We do not use persistent cross-site tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or behavioral profiling scripts.

5. Third-Party Hosting

The static web assets for WebDrop Pro are hosted via Cloudflare Pages. Standard anonymous web traffic metadata (such as browser type and IP address) may be processed by hosting infrastructure providers strictly for service security, DDoS protection, and performance optimization. This metadata is not used to build individual profiles.

6. WebRTC STUN Servers

To establish browser-to-browser connections across different networks, WebDrop Pro uses public Google STUN servers (stun.l.google.com) for the purpose of NAT traversal. STUN servers only see your IP address during the connection setup phase and do not receive any file contents or message payloads. The actual data channel is end-to-end encrypted between the two peers.

7. No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or share any data collected through WebDrop Pro with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. The service has no advertising integrations and no third-party analytics scripts embedded in the application interface.

8. Data Retention

Local browser storage persists only on the device where you set your preferences. QR pairing session records expire automatically after 15 minutes. No long-term data store is maintained that links your device to file transfer activity.

9. Your Rights

Because we do not maintain personal accounts or persistent personal data stores, formal data subject access requests are not required. You may clear all locally stored preferences at any time through your browser's site data controls.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of WebDrop Pro after any change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact
